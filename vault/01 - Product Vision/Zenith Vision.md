# Zenith — Product Vision

> **Small steps, every day.**

Zenith helps people build and keep good habits with the least possible friction.
Open it, tap a circle, feel the momentum. No accounts, no nagging, no clutter.

## Who it's for
Busy people who have tried five other habit apps and abandoned them because they
were *too much*. Zenith is deliberately tiny.

## Principles
1. **One tap to log.** Marking a habit done today is a single action.
2. **Momentum is the product.** Streaks, heatmaps, and gentle stats make progress visible.
3. **Local-first & private.** Everything lives in the browser. No sign-up.
4. **Calm design.** Dark, quiet, focused. Never gamified to the point of guilt.

## Where requirements come from
Product ideas are captured in [[../09 - How it works/SDLC Pipeline|the pipeline]] as
**requirement notes** in `02 - Requirements`. When a requirement is marked
`status: ready`, the autonomous pipeline grooms it into GitHub issues and builds it.

## Roadmap themes
- **Momentum** — streaks, heatmaps, best-streak records.
- **Insight** — weekly/monthly completion stats.
- **Delight** — themes, celebratory micro-animations.
- **Portability** — export / import data as JSON.
